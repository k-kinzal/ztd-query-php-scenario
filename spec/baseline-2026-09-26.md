### What the differences mean

The full SQLite suite is not green. Its existing expectations include historical bugs, incomplete test setup, and current upstream problems. A failure count alone cannot establish a regression in the library.

- **Newly supported behavior / outdated expectations:** `SqliteAlterAddColumnDmlTest` still expects new-column reads to throw, but those operations now succeed. Upstream [#54](https://github.com/k-kinzal/ztd-query-php/issues/54) is closed as completed. Several subquery and binary-data tests similarly expect errors that no longer occur.
- **Test infrastructure defects:** `SqliteChainedDdlDmlTest` supplies invalid DDL to raw PDO before ZTD is constructed; `SqliteCheckConstraintBehaviorTest` has literal backslashes in its raw SQL; `SqliteConcurrentInstancesTest` accesses an uninitialized connection. These are scenario-suite defects and are not counted as confirmed upstream bugs.
- **Confirmed upstream problem:** `SqliteColumnOrderInsertTest::testPreparedWithReorderedColumns` assigns positional parameter values to the wrong columns. Native PDO returns `id=10, name=PrepItem, price=19.99`; ZTD returns `id=19, name=10, price=0`. This was independently reproduced against native PDO and reported as [#465](https://github.com/k-kinzal/ztd-query-php/issues/465) after checking existing open and closed issues. The existing test remains a failing regression scenario.

The numbered specifications and traceability statuses retain their explicitly labeled v0.1.1 observations. Their database columns now follow current support; retained historical cells were checked against the previous files. Those cells do not claim verification on the new commit. The superseded monolithic spec and dated verification summary retain their historical versions.
